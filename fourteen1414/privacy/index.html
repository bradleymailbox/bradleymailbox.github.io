Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · Android app by bDevStudios

Fourteen1414 is a card game. It has no accounts and no analytics of its own, and bDevStudios does not collect, receive or sell any information about you. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some information as described below. Buying Premium turns off the ads between games.

What the game keeps on your phone

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things on your device only:

  • the name you type for high scores and multiplayer
  • your scores, leaderboard, stats, achievements and daily streak
  • saved jokers and gems
  • your settings (theme, music, sound, vibration) and whether you own Premium
  • if you're in a local multiplayer match: the room code, the host phone's local network address and a random code, so you can rejoin if the app closes

None of this is sent to bDevStudios. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

Android's own backup can include this saved game data in your device backup, which is controlled by your Google account settings. bDevStudios has no access to those backups.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a full-screen ad between some games, and offers an optional video ad that pays gems. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the game collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • information about your device and the app, and how you interact with ads
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data

bDevStudios does not see this information. Google's use of it is covered by Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the EU), the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings (under Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads).

Purchases

Premium is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; bDevStudios never sees your payment details. The game asks Google Play only whether your Google account owns Premium, so that it can turn it on, including after a reinstall or on a new phone.

Local multiplayer

Local multiplayer connects phones directly over the same Wi-Fi network or a phone's hotspot. It does not use the internet or any bDevStudios server, and no ads are shown during a match. During a match, the phones in the room share:

  • each player's name
  • the moves each player makes, their score and their progress

This information goes only to the other phones in the room and is not stored once you leave the match (apart from the rejoin details above, which are cleared when you leave).
