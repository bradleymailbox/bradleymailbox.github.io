About Fourteen1414

Fourteen1414 is a bright, fast take on Fourteen Out solitaire. Tap two cards that add up to 14 (J is 11, Q is 12, K is 13) and clear the deck.

Chain your pairs for combos, play the daily game, or play against friends.

Highlights

  • Chain combos and double-points boosts
  • Jokers to rescue a stuck deal
  • A daily game with the same cards for everyone
  • Local multiplayer for 2 to 4 phones on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot
  • Achievements to collect
