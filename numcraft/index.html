About NumCraft

NumCraft is a numbers puzzle. You are dealt a set of numbers and a target, and you build a formula that hits it.

Harder levels score more. Earn experience, climb the ranks and try the daily challenge.

Highlights

  • Easy, Medium, Hard and Random levels
  • A daily challenge worth bonus points
  • Golden numbers for bonus points
  • Hints when you get stuck
  • Ranks, streaks and a high-score board
