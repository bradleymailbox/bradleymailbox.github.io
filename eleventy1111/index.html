About Eleventy1111

Eleventy1111 is a fast, colourful take on Elevens solitaire. Pair cards that make 11, chain them into combos and clear the deck.

Play solo, take on the daily deal, or play against friends on the same Wi-Fi.

Highlights

  • Combos that build up to mega combos
  • Jokers and shuffles to get you out of a tight spot
  • A daily deal with the same cards for everyone
  • Local multiplayer over Wi-Fi
  • Achievements and card themes
