Eleventy1111
Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026 · Android app by bDevStudios

Eleventy1111 is a card game. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and bDevStudios does not collect, receive or sell any information about you.

What the game keeps on your phone

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things on your device only:

  • the name you type for high scores and multiplayer
  • your scores, leaderboard, stats, achievements and daily streak
  • saved jokers and free deals
  • your settings (theme, music, sound, vibration)
  • if you're in a local multiplayer match: the room code, the host phone's local network address and a random code, so you can rejoin if the app closes

None of this is sent to bDevStudios. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

Android's own backup can include this saved game data in your device backup, which is controlled by your Google account settings. bDevStudios has no access to those backups.

Local multiplayer

Local multiplayer connects phones directly over the same Wi-Fi network or a phone's hotspot. It does not use the internet or any bDevStudios server. During a match, the phones in the room share:

  • each player's name
  • the moves each player makes, their score and their progress

This information goes only to the other phones in the room and is not stored once you leave the match (apart from the rejoin details above, which are cleared when you leave).
