About Go Cart Junkie

Go Cart Junkie is retro top-down kart racing. Drift for mini-turbos, slipstream your rivals and fight your way up through the field.

Spend your winnings in the garage and the cart shop, then take on the career, daily challenges and quick races.

Highlights

  • Eight circuits, including desert, snow and night races
  • Drift mini-turbos and slipstreaming
  • A career full of rivals
  • Daily challenges
  • A garage of carts to upgrade, from engine and tyres to brakes and boost
