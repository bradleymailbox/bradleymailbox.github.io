About Cube Rush

Cube Rush is a one-touch arcade runner. Jump, dodge and climb through four worlds full of falling boulders, saws, pits and spikes.

Unlock all 54 tiles of the cube, then see how far you can go in Endless Run.

Highlights

  • Four worlds to explore
  • Power-ups that matter, like a shield that blocks the next hit
  • 54 cube tiles to unlock
  • Endless Run mode with a best distance to beat
