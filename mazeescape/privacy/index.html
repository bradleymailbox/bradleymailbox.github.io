Maze Escape
Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026 · Android app by bDevStudios

Maze Escape is a maze and trivia game. It has no accounts and no analytics of its own, and bDevStudios does not collect, receive or sell any information about you. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some information as described below. Buying Premium turns off the ads between levels.

What the game keeps on your phone

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things on your device only:

  • the name you type for the high-score list
  • your scores, unlocked levels and stars, daily maze results and endless-mode bests
  • your settings (theme, maze style, music, sound effects, reduce motion) and whether you own Premium

None of this is sent to bDevStudios. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

Android's own backup can include this saved game data in your device backup, which is controlled by your Google account settings. bDevStudios has no access to those backups.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a full-screen ad between some levels, and offers an optional video ad that gives you an extra life after a game over. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the game collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • information about your device and the app, and how you interact with ads
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data

bDevStudios does not see this information. Google's use of it is covered by Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the EU), the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Options › Accessibility › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings (under Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads).

Google Play Games (worldwide leaderboard)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the score from a finished campaign run is posted to the game's worldwide leaderboard, where other players can see it with your Play Games gamer name and avatar. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and your Play Games profile settings. The game works fully without signing in.

Purchases

Premium is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; bDevStudios never sees your payment details. The game asks Google Play only whether your Google account owns Premium, so that it can turn it on, including after a reinstall or on a new phone.
