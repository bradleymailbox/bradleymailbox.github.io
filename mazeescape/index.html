About Maze Escape

Maze Escape mixes neon mazes with trivia. Race the clock, collect every question in the maze, answer it, and find your way out.

Work through the campaign, try the daily maze, or see how long you can last in endless mode.

Highlights

  • A 50-level campaign with stars to earn
  • A new daily maze every day
  • Endless mode
  • A worldwide leaderboard through Google Play Games
