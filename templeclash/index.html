About Temple Clash

Temple Clash is an original tactical board game. Two temple clans clash on a 7×7 battlefield, and every move is made with a Technique card.

Play the AI at four levels, or play a friend over local Wi-Fi.

Highlights

  • Quick, tactical matches on a 7×7 board
  • Technique cards that decide how your pieces can move
  • An AI opponent at four levels
  • Local Wi-Fi multiplayer
  • Board themes from Temple Sand and Ink & Paper to Night Shrine and Cherry Blossom
