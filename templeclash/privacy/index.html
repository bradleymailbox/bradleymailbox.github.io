Temple Clash
Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · Android app by bDevStudios

Temple Clash is a strategy board game. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and bDevStudios does not collect, receive or sell any information about you. The game never connects to a bDevStudios server or any other internet service.

What the game keeps on your phone

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things on your device only:

  • the name you use for local multiplayer (it starts as your device's name, and you can change it)
  • your battle history: results, opponents' names, Champions used and match lengths
  • your settings (board theme, music, sound effects, vibration and animations)

None of this is sent to bDevStudios. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

Android's own backup can include this saved data in your device backup, which is controlled by your Google account settings. bDevStudios has no access to those backups.

Local multiplayer

Local multiplayer connects two phones directly over the same Wi-Fi network or a phone's hotspot. It does not use the internet or any bDevStudios server.

  • While a room is open, the host phone announces it on the local network with its room code and the host's player name, so the other phone can find it.
  • During a match the two phones share each player's name, their chosen Champion and every move.

This information goes only to devices on the same local network and is not stored once the match ends, apart from the result in each player's own battle history.
